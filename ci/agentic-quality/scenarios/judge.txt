## Scoring Rubric (each dimension 1–10)

| Dimension | 1 (worst) | 5 (adequate) | 10 (best) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **novelty** | Trivial tweak (e.g., change lr from 1e-3 to 5e-4) | Sensible but common approach | Genuinely novel angle not commonly tried |
| **feasibility** | Physically impossible or requires unavailable data | Requires minor setup | Drop-in implementable in one session |
| **expected_gain_pp** | Likely hurts or <0.5 pp | ~1–2 pp expected gain | >5 pp plausible gain with evidence |
| **specificity** | Vague ("use a better optimizer") | Somewhat specified | Precisely specified with hyperparams/citation |
| **citation_quality** | No citations | Some citations, not verified | ≥2 authoritative citations with URLs |

## Judge Integrity Rules

1. Be specific — quote the proposal when citing a strength or weakness.
2. Flag degenerate proposals — if a proposal would game the metric (e.g., predict-all-positive to maximize recall), list it in red_flags.
3. Do not inflate scores — a score of 8+ requires concrete evidence or citation.
4. Composite score = novelty*0.25 + feasibility*0.20 + expected_gain_pp*0.30 + specificity*0.15 + citation_quality*0.10 (compute precisely).
5. Return valid JSON only — no prose outside the JSON block.
